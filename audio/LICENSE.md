# Sounds

Field recordings from BigSoundBank (https://bigsoundbank.com, run by Joseph Sardin), released as CC0 1.0
(public domain; no attribution required, credited anyway). Sources by BigSoundBank number:
Mountain stream #1 (2754), Small stream #4 (1354), Big branching fire #3 (0989), Wind in the trees (0904),
Field cricket (1020), Nocturnal insect #3 (0426), Tawny owls (0429), Dogs breathing (1547), Old dog barking #2 (2353).

Changes: loops cut and crossfaded, calls split, normalised, mono AAC (scripts/prepare-sounds.py).
