The corner solution, and the eleven orders of magnitude between presets

Total work against the eager/lazy split, with the read/write ratio as the dial. Try the presets in order: add a secondary index has a break-even of 0.00002 and choosing lazy costs 45,000×; fan out one post to 50M followers has a break-even of 5,000,000 and the same choice is right in the other direction. Then watch the curve itself — it is monotone in the split, so the best answer sits at an end, and the readouts tell you which end and by how much.
